About

DEDICATION TO DELIVERING BEST FIRE SAFETY SOLUTIONS

At RONAK Fire India Pvt. Ltd., we are dedicated to the science, engineering, and advancement of modern firefighting solutions. Established with a vision to become India’s most trusted and recognized fire protection brand, we specialize in manufacturing high-quality firefighting equipment, fire protection systems, and fire alarm solutions under one integrated group.

The company is led by an Ex-Defence professional with more than 20 years of industry experience, driving innovation, discipline, and excellence across all operations. Supported by a highly skilled technical team, we design and manufacture products that conform to stringent I.S.I. standards, ensuring reliability and safety at every stage.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is equipped with modern infrastructure, allowing us to continuously enhance product performance, efficiency, and safety. We follow a rigorous ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System, certified by JAS-ANZ, ensuring consistent quality control—from raw material selection to final dispatch.

Driven by customer-centric policies and a philosophy that only the best products leave our plant, RONAK has earned the trust of a prestigious clientele that includes leading national and multinational organizations, along with government establishments across India.

At RONAK, we are committed to protecting life and property through innovation, precision engineering, and uncompromising quality.

Vision

To become India’s most trusted and technologically advanced fire protection brand—delivering world-class firefighting products and integrated safety solutions that protect life, property, and critical infrastructure with uncompromising quality, consistency, and innovation.

Mission

  • Delivering top-quality fire protection systems that safeguard lives and assets.
  • To continuously innovate and upgrade safety systems aligned with evolving fire regulations and industry demands.
  • To ensure timely project execution with precision, transparency, and efficiency.
  • To provide exceptional customer support and technical guidance throughout the lifecycle of our products.
  • To build safer environments for industries, infrastructure, and communities nationwide.

Manufacturing Unit

Our factory comprises multiple departments that produce components, assemble and test finished products:

  • Press Shop
  • Trop (rod & pipe cutting)
  • Welding & Fabrication
  • Lathe & CNC
  • Phosphating & Powder Coating
  • Aluminium Furnace & Casting
  • Sprinkler Assembly & Testing
  • Cylinder Filling & Testing
  • Assembly & Dispatch
  • Box Department

The Press Shop is where raw metal sheets are transformed into precise components using hydraulic and power presses. Every cut, bend, and punch is executed with accuracy to create the foundational parts for hose reels, boxes, and structural assemblies. It’s the first stage that shapes the strength and quality of our final products.
