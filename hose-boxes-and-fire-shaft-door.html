Hose Boxes & Fire Shaft Door

Hose Boxes are essential enclosures installed along fire escape routes and near hydrant points to safely protect and store firefighting hose pipes, branch pipes, and nozzles.

Fabricated from mild steel (MS) with heavy-duty epoxy powder coating, they provide excellent corrosion resistance, impact protection, and long-term durability.

Available in single-door and double-door configurations depending on building risk level and storage capacity.

General Performance Features

All our hose boxes provide:

  • Strong mechanical protection for equipment
  • Corrosion resistance in moist staircases/basements
  • Abrasion-resistant coating
  • Tamper-proof lock options
  • Smooth edges to avoid hose cuts
Enquiry

Surface Treatment

  • High-quality epoxy powder coat
  • Standard Post Office (PO) Red
  • Uniform coating inside & outside to prevent rust formation
  • UV-resistance for outdoor longevity

Installation Guidelines

  • Mount at 1.0–1.2 m height for ergonomic access
  • Position near hydrant landing valves
  • Ensure clear floor space for hose deployment

Optional Add-Ons

  • Transparent glass/acrylic viewing window
  • Stainless steel variants (for corrosive environments)
  • Customized sizes on request
  • Branding / equipment labelling
  • Weatherproof canopy top

Single Door Hose Box

Premium variant with corrosion-resistant chrome plated elbow:

Designed to house:

  • 1× 15-meter hose pipe
  • 1× branch pipe/nozzle

Ideal for:

  • Low-risk occupancy areas
