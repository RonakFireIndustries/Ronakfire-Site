Hose Reel Drums (HRD)

A first-aid fire-fighting appliance connected to a building’s pressurized water source. It provides a continuous and controllable jet/spray pattern through its shut-off nozzle for tackling early-stage Class A fires.

Standard Specifications:

  • Hose Length: 30 meters (Semi-rigid)
  • Operating Pressure: 7–12 bar
  • Hose Size: ¾” or 1” (as per design)
  • Mounting Height: 1.2–1.5 m (per code)
  • Finish: UV-resistant powder-coated for durability
  • Shut-Off Nozzle: Jet/Spray control (lockable optional)

Ideal for: Offices, malls, industries, educational institutes, hospitals, basements, parking areas, staircases.
