RONAK RRL Fire Hose (Rubber Reinforced Lined)

Conforming to IS:636/1988 Type-A, ISI Marked CE Compliant

The RONAK RRL Hose is designed for high-performance water delivery in firefighting and civil engineering applications. It combines durability, flexibility, and high-pressure resistance suitable for industrial and commercial environments.

Standard Lengths

  • 15 meters
  • 22.5 meters
  • 30 meters
  • 75 meters
(Custom lengths available on request)

End Connections

  • Supplied with couplings as per customer requirement
  • Available in Stainless Steel or Gunmetal instantaneous type

Key Features

  • Compact, flexible, and lightweight construction
  • High abrasion resistance
  • Reinforced Rubber Lining (RRL) design
  • Capable of withstanding high operating and back pressure
  • Treated to resist mildew and moisture damage
  • Rot-proof outer surface
  • Suitable for frequent deployment and rough handling
  • CE certified for performance and safety

Applications

  • Fire hydrant system water delivery
  • Industrial fire stations
  • Civil engineering projects
  • Warehouses, logistics hubs, factories
  • Municipal fire brigade usage
  • Refineries, chemical premises, port facilities

Construction Details

Jacket

  • Circular woven jacket
  • Manufactured from a special blend of cotton and synthetic yarn
  • High abrasion and heat resistance
  • Treated with rot-proofing agents to prevent fungus and decay

Reinforcement

  • Thin reinforcing fabric bonded using a high-strength adhesive
  • Increases pressure-handling capacity
  • Enhances structural stability

Rubber Lining

  • Specially compounded rubber inner lining
  • Provides high flexibility and durability
  • Resistant to cracking, wear, and friction loss
  • Ensures smooth water flow and long service life

Operating Characteristics

  • Suitable for continuous water flow conditions
  • Able to withstand surge and back pressure
  • Maintains structural integrity under rigorous usage
  • Performs reliably in varying climatic conditions

Packaging & Identification

  • Supplied rolled for compact storage
  • Product size and specifications printed on the jacket
  • Color coding available on request

Compliance & Standards

  • Manufactured as per IS:636 Type-A
  • CE Approved
  • Complies with National Building Code guidelines
  • Accepted by fire department authorities for approval processes

Optional Accessories

  • Branch pipe and nozzle set
  • Stainless steel or gunmetal couplings
  • Hose ramp / bridge for vehicle crossing
  • Wall-mounted hose rack

Thermoplastic Fire Hose (High-Pressure Firefighting Delivery Hose)

The Thermoplastic Fire Hose is designed for rapid and reliable water delivery in firefighting operations. Manufactured from high-quality virgin thermoplastic compounds, it delivers superior flexibility, flame resistance, and durability across industrial, commercial, and residential applications.

Available Lengths

  • 30 meters
  • 60 meters
(Custom lengths available upon request)

Available Sizes

  • 12.5 mm and 25 mm nominal bore

Material Construction

  • Made from high-quality virgin thermoplastic compound
  • Inner and outer layers designed for abrasion and climatic resistance

Reinforcement Layer

  • High-pressure nylon yarn reinforcement
  • Enhances burst strength and stability under dynamic loads

Bend Radius

  • Can operate at a radius of approximately 5 times the nominal bore size
  • Meets BIS compliance for flexibility and manoeuvrability

Working Temperature

  • Short-term resistance up to 150°F (65°C)
  • Recommended continuous operation at 110°F (43°C)
  • Thermal expansion controlled via multi-layer construction

Impact Resistance

  • High impact-resistant surface
  • Does not crack or break easily under sudden force

Chemical Resistance

  • Resistant to acids, alkalis, and most inorganic chemicals
  • Suitable for industrial and chemical zone deployment

Jacket

  • Inherently superior flame-retardant characteristics due to chlorine content
  • High ignition temperature: approx. 455°C
  • Self-extinguishing material — does not support continuous combustion
  • Reduced risk of fire propagation in storage zones

Key Performance Features

  • Lightweight design for rapid hose deployment
  • Enhanced UV stability for outdoor installations
  • Smooth inner lining for optimized water flow rates
  • Resistant to mildew and ambient moisture

Recommended Applications

  • Fire hydrant systems
  • Industrial plants & battery units
  • Warehouses & logistics terminals
  • Commercial complexes
  • Chemical manufacturing facilities
  • Temporary firefighting setups

Testing & Compliance

All hoses should be:

  • Hydro-tested as per relevant BIS/IS guidelines
  • Inspected for pressure retention
  • Verified for burst pressure rating at installation

Advantages Over Conventional Rubber Hoses

  • Higher chemical resistance
  • Better flame retardancy
  • Reduced weight & coil memory
  • Extended service life

Optional Accessories

  • Instantaneous couplings (GM/SS)
  • Multipurpose spray/jet nozzles
  • Wall-mounted hose racks
  • Hose bridge ramps
